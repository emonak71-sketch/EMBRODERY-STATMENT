# Embroidery Statement v1.0.7

- Owner Control Center automatically lists registered customer Gmail accounts.
- Select a customer to save or update their mobile number and manage access.
- The customer list refreshes after saving a mobile number.
- Customer accounts cannot access the owner directory, phone lookup or phone editing.
- Gmail remains the customer login; saved numbers retrieve the matching Gmail.
- Customer and Owner Windows installers retain their existing application identifiers, updater channels and signing key configuration.
- Includes the dependency lockfile repair for reproducible builds.
