What is Embroidery Statement?

Embroidery Statement is business management software for embroidery businesses. It helps organise customer records, account ledgers, statements, payments, outstanding balances, worker attendance and reports.

Can I track customer payments and outstanding dues?

Yes. You can record customer transactions and payments, review account activity and check outstanding balances.
