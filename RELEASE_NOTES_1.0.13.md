# Embroidery Statement 1.0.13

- Salary Increase is now a direct tab under Workers & Payroll.
- Enter the new total monthly salary and effective date; preview the increase before saving. Reason and notes are optional.
- Shows current salary, last increase, next annual review, the 15-day reminder date and a phone-friendly increase history on one page.
- Salary changes only after the user saves. No automatic salary amount changes.
- Keeps existing account controls, signing identities and updater compatibility.
